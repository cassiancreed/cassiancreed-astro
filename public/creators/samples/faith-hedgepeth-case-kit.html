The one-minute brief

  • Faith Hedgepeth was a 19-year-old student at UNC-Chapel Hill. She was found dead in an off-campus apartment on Sept 7, 2012. Miguel Enrique Salguero-Olivares is on trial in her death. The trial is in Durham County Superior Court.
  • He is charged with four first-degree crimes: murder, burglary, rape and sexual offense. His lawyers entered pleas of “not guilty of all charges” on Aug 3, 2026. He is presumed innocent. There is no verdict.
  • Both sides have rested (finished presenting evidence). The state rested Oct 19 after nine days and 23 witnesses. On Oct 21, he told the judge he would not testify, and the defense called no witnesses.
  • Closing arguments (each side’s final speech to the jury) begin Thu, Oct 22, at 9:30 a.m. ET (6:30 a.m. PT). Then the case goes to the jury. No verdict date is set.
  • The DNA link and the “stranger” claim come from prosecutors. Neither is a court finding. The defense says the DNA does not show when or how it was left.

Name and fact checks

  • Victim: Faith Hedgepeth. CBS17 (2021) gives her full name as Faith Danielle Hedgepeth. She was 19 (N&O; AP).
  • Defendant: CBS17 gives his name as Miguel Enrique Salguero-Olivares. This kit uses that spelling. The N&O, Herald-Sun and WRAL also write “Miguel Salguero Olivares” (no hyphen) or “Olivares.”
  • Defendant’s age: 28 at his Sept 2021 arrest (CBS17); 33 in Oct 2026 coverage (Fox; N&O). The N&O says he was 19 in 2012. N.C. law treats people under 18 at the time of a murder under a separate part of the law.
  • Prosecutor: “Garcia-Lamarca” (AP; N&O) vs. “Garcia-LaMarca” (WRAL).
  • SBI agent: “Heinrich” (WRAL; Herald-Sun). One ABC11 entry spells it “Heimrich.”
  • Place: the apartment is on the Chapel Hill / Durham County line. Most outlets say “Chapel Hill.” WRAL and the N&O (2021) place it in Durham County. The trial is in Durham because the apartment is in Durham County.
  • Outlets describe how his DNA sample was obtained in 2021 in different ways. AP (Oct 7, 2026) says only that a sample sent to the state lab matched the profile from the scene.
  • Date of the added charges: not verified. The N&O’s Oct 7, 2026 timeline says October 2024. ABC11’s story is dated Nov 14, 2024 (updated Nov 15). A story date is not an indictment date.
  • Prior record: reports differ. The N&O (Oct 7, 2026) says he had no prior criminal convictions. CBS17 (Sept 17, 2021) says prosecutors described a previous DWI (driving while impaired) conviction and no previous violent-crime convictions. His record was not checked against court records. The N&O also reports no known connection between him and Faith Hedgepeth.
  • Outlets disagree on the plea date. ABC11 and the N&O say Aug 3, 2026, and ABC11 says that was the first time pleas were entered in open court. East Idaho News (Oct 9, 2026) says he “pleaded not guilty in November 2024.”
  • Time zone: court times in Durham are Eastern. 9:30 a.m. ET is 6:30 a.m. PT.
  • A Herald-Sun live blog has a typo, “Sept 12, 20212.” The date of death is Sept 7, 2012 (N&O).
  • Witness count: WRAL and ABC11 say 23 witnesses over nine days. Chapelboro’s shorter list is partial, not a conflict.
