Today at ten fifteen, Pacific time, the Idaho Supreme Court hears Lori Vallow Daybell's appeal. First, the victims: her son, seven. Her daughter, sixteen. And Tammy Daybell, forty-nine. In twenty twenty-three, a jury convicted her of first-degree murder in the deaths of her son and daughter. It also convicted her of conspiring to murder Tammy Daybell. She is serving life without parole. Today is a review, not a new trial. No witnesses testify. Her attorneys say the judge wrongly removed the attorney she hired, and that she was denied a speedy trial. The State asks the court to keep her convictions in place. The justices rule later, in writing. Until then, her convictions stand. Court dates and sources: Cassy-an Creed dot com, court calendar.
