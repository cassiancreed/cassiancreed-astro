**A Los Angeles judge ordered David Anthony Burke — the musician known as D4vd — to stand trial on July 27, 2026, at the close of a five-day preliminary hearing.** He has pleaded not guilty. The judge found that the evidence presented was sufficient for the charges to move forward. That is all the ruling decides. It is not a verdict, it is not a finding of guilt, and Burke remains presumed innocent unless and until he is proved guilty in court ([Los Angeles County District Attorney](https://da.lacounty.gov/media/news/d4vd-ordered-stand-trial-murdering-dismembering-14-year-old-girl)).

Celeste Rivas Hernandez was 14 years old.

## What happened in court

The preliminary hearing ran five days in Los Angeles County Superior Court. Prosecutors called a dozen witnesses and laid out the framework of their case; they did not present everything they have, and were not required to ([Los Angeles County District Attorney](https://da.lacounty.gov/media/news/d4vd-ordered-stand-trial-murdering-dismembering-14-year-old-girl)).

Judge Charlaine Olmedo ruled that the evidence gave probable cause to proceed on all three charges: murder, continuous sexual abuse of a child under 14, and unlawful mutilation of human remains. She also found probable cause for three special-circumstance allegations — lying in wait, killing for financial gain, and killing a potential witness — which is what makes the case death-penalty eligible under California law. Prosecutors have said the decision on whether to seek the death penalty will be made later. Burke was ordered held without bail ([AP](https://apnews.com/article/52aced35d8cccc3a3d6445b6d13db6a5)).

Defense lawyers argued the murder evidence was insufficient. They did not contest the other two charges at this stage ([AP](https://apnews.com/article/52aced35d8cccc3a3d6445b6d13db6a5)).

## What evidence was presented

Testimony across the hearing fell into a handful of categories. We describe them at the level the record supports, and no further.

- **Forensic and DNA evidence.** Los Angeles police criminalists testified about apparent stains recovered from a rubber floor mat, a rowing machine and a charger in the garage of Burke's Hollywood home, and from the Tesla registered to him. A police DNA specialist testified that several of those samples were a strong match to Celeste Rivas Hernandez's profile ([AP](https://apnews.com/article/65647a24c7808bebc0b6ff9040f50615)).
- **Digital communications and account records.** Prosecutors introduced text messages between Burke and Rivas Hernandez from the days and hours before her death, which they say establish their motive theory ([Los Angeles County District Attorney](https://da.lacounty.gov/media/news/d4vd-ordered-stand-trial-murdering-dismembering-14-year-old-girl)).
- **Purchase records.** Prosecutors presented records they say show orders — some placed under an alias — for a shovel, chain saws, a body bag and an inflatable pool in the days after April 23, 2025 ([Los Angeles County District Attorney](https://da.lacounty.gov/media/news/d4vd-ordered-stand-trial-murdering-dismembering-14-year-old-girl)).
- **Vehicle and location evidence.** Rivas Hernandez's remains were found on September 8, 2025 in the front trunk of a Tesla registered to Burke, after the vehicle was reported abandoned and impounded. A California Highway Patrol officer testified that a road worker found her passport in a rural part of Santa Barbara County in January; prosecutors said data placed Burke in that area ([Los Angeles County District Attorney](https://da.lacounty.gov/media/news/d4vd-ordered-stand-trial-murdering-dismembering-14-year-old-girl); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/32ca2cc61ca6b7db8e3170888d3dacd4)).
- **Witness and investigator testimony.** A homicide detective, multiple criminalists and financial witnesses testified. The financial testimony went to the prosecution's theory that Burke had a career and income he stood to lose ([AP](https://apnews.com/article/32ca2cc61ca6b7db8e3170888d3dacd4)).

Everything in that list is evidence a judge found sufficient to send the case forward. None of it has been tested by a jury, and a preliminary hearing is not the forum where guilt is determined.

## What the defense argued

Burke's lawyers made three broad arguments, and it matters that they are recorded as arguments rather than findings.

**Intent.** Attorney Marilyn Bednarski told the court there was no evidence Burke "harbored a deliberate intent to kill" Rivas Hernandez, and argued the communications showed no threat and no history of violence — that malice, the mental state a murder charge requires, had not been shown ([AP](https://apnews.com/article/52aced35d8cccc3a3d6445b6d13db6a5)).

**Interpretation of the forensic evidence.** Under cross-examination, the prosecution's DNA analyst acknowledged she could not say with certainty that the DNA profiles came from blood rather than touch, that it cannot be determined how DNA arrived on a given spot, that DNA can transfer between people, and that there is no way to establish how long it had been there ([AP](https://apnews.com/article/65647a24c7808bebc0b6ff9040f50615)).

**Collection and testing.** Attorney Blair Berk pressed criminalists on how evidence was collected and preserved — who wore gloves around the car, why the exterior of the Tesla was not swabbed, and why items that tested negative for blood were not also swabbed for touch DNA ([AP](https://apnews.com/article/32ca2cc61ca6b7db8e3170888d3dacd4)).

**Sufficiency.** The defense also argued that the medical examiner's homicide determination was too inconclusive to support a murder charge. The defense did not offer an alternative account of how Rivas Hernandez died, and is not required to ([AP](https://apnews.com/article/52aced35d8cccc3a3d6445b6d13db6a5)).

The judge heard those arguments and still found probable cause. That does not mean the arguments were rejected as false — only that they did not defeat the lower standard that applies at this stage.

## What the judge actually decided

A preliminary hearing answers one question: is there enough evidence for the case to go to trial?

The standard is probable cause. In plain terms, the judge has to decide whether a reasonable person could conclude, on the evidence presented, that the charged offenses occurred and that this defendant committed them. It is a screening test, not a verdict.

That standard is deliberately far below the one a jury will apply. At trial, the prosecution must prove each element beyond a reasonable doubt, to twelve jurors, unanimously. At a preliminary hearing, the judge does not weigh which side is more convincing, does not resolve conflicts in the evidence in the way a jury would, and does not decide guilt.

So the honest reading of July 27 is narrow: **the case survived. Nothing was proved.** Judge Olmedo did not find that Burke is guilty, and did not find that the prosecution proved its case.

## What happens next

- **Post-preliminary arraignment — Monday, August 31, 2026,** in Department 105 of the Clara Shortridge Foltz Criminal Justice Center in Los Angeles. This is where Burke is arraigned on the information, the charging document that follows a holding order ([Los Angeles County District Attorney](https://da.lacounty.gov/media/news/d4vd-ordered-stand-trial-murdering-dismembering-14-year-old-girl)).
- **Pretrial motions and scheduling** would normally follow. No trial date has been set. AP reported that a trial could occur as soon as 60 days after arraignment; treat that as an outer possibility, not a schedule ([AP](https://apnews.com/article/52aced35d8cccc3a3d6445b6d13db6a5)).
- **A death-penalty decision** has not been made. Prosecutors have said it will come later ([Los Angeles County District Attorney](https://da.lacounty.gov/media/news/d4vd-ordered-stand-trial-murdering-dismembering-14-year-old-girl)).

## Frequently asked questions

**Does being ordered to stand trial mean D4vd was found guilty?**
No. A holding order means a judge found enough evidence for the charges to proceed to trial. It is not a conviction and it decides no one's guilt. Burke has pleaded not guilty and is presumed innocent unless and until proved guilty.

**What is the standard at a preliminary hearing?**
Probable cause — whether a reasonable person could conclude from the evidence presented that the offenses occurred and the defendant committed them. It is substantially lower than the beyond-a-reasonable-doubt standard a jury applies at trial.

**What is D4vd charged with?**
One count each of murder, continuous sexual abuse of a child under the age of 14, and unlawful mutilation of human remains, along with special-circumstance allegations of lying in wait, murder for financial gain, and murder of a witness, and a special allegation of personal use of a deadly and dangerous weapon. All of it is an allegation.

**When is his next court date?**
A post-preliminary arraignment is scheduled for August 31, 2026 in Department 105 of the Foltz Criminal Justice Center in Los Angeles. No trial date has been set.

**Is the death penalty on the table?**
He is eligible for it because the judge found probable cause for the special circumstances. Prosecutors have not decided whether they will seek it.
