A Florida judge sentenced Tomasz Kosowski to death on Nov. 20, 2026. Judge Joseph Bulone had found him guilty in September of the first-degree murder of Steven Cozzi, a Largo lawyer. Kosowski is a former plastic surgeon. He chose to represent himself. The sentence can be appealed.

## Steven Cozzi first

Steven Cozzi was 41. He was a lawyer at Blanchard Law in Largo, Florida ([ABA Journal](https://abajournal.com/news/article/plastic-surgeon-sentenced-to-death-for-killing-beloved-lawyer); [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/US/florida-plastic-surgeon-sentenced-death-murdering-missing-lawyer/story?id=138327475)). He vanished from his office in March 2023. His body has never been found ([ABC News](https://abcnews.com/US/florida-plastic-surgeon-sentenced-death-murdering-missing-lawyer/story?id=138327475); [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/us/florida-man-sentenced-death-cold-calculated-murder-attorney-body-never-found.amp); [ABA Journal](https://abajournal.com/news/article/plastic-surgeon-sentenced-to-death-for-killing-beloved-lawyer)).

## How the case got here

- **The dispute.** Steven Cozzi represented people Kosowski had sued in a fight over medical billing money ([ABC News](https://abcnews.com/US/florida-plastic-surgeon-sentenced-death-murdering-missing-lawyer/story?id=138327475); [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/us/florida-man-sentenced-death-cold-calculated-murder-attorney-body-never-found.amp); [ABA Journal](https://abajournal.com/news/article/plastic-surgeon-sentenced-to-death-for-killing-beloved-lawyer)).
- **The trial.** Kosowski gave up his right to a jury. That made it a bench trial, where the judge alone decides. He acted as his own lawyer ([ABC News](https://abcnews.com/US/florida-plastic-surgeon-sentenced-death-murdering-missing-lawyer/story?id=138327475); [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/us/florida-man-sentenced-death-cold-calculated-murder-attorney-body-never-found.amp)).
- **The verdict.** Judge Bulone found him guilty of first-degree murder in September 2026 ([ABC News](https://abcnews.com/US/florida-plastic-surgeon-sentenced-death-murdering-missing-lawyer/story?id=138327475); [ABA Journal](https://abajournal.com/news/article/plastic-surgeon-sentenced-to-death-for-killing-beloved-lawyer)).

## The evidence the judge heard

These were the state's points at trial. The judge accepted the state's case when he found Kosowski guilty.

- **Blood.** Prosecutors pointed to Steven Cozzi's blood in the office bathroom and in Kosowski's truck ([ABC News](https://abcnews.com/US/florida-plastic-surgeon-sentenced-death-murdering-missing-lawyer/story?id=138327475); [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/us/florida-man-sentenced-death-cold-calculated-murder-attorney-body-never-found.amp)).
- **Video.** Surveillance video showed Kosowski going in with a large box. He left about two hours later pulling a heavy cart or wagon ([ABA Journal](https://abajournal.com/news/article/plastic-surgeon-sentenced-to-death-for-killing-beloved-lawyer); [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/US/florida-plastic-surgeon-sentenced-death-murdering-missing-lawyer/story?id=138327475)).
- **The search.** Prosecutors said the body went into a dumpster that was taken to a landfill. Investigators searched the landfill ([ABC News](https://abcnews.com/US/florida-plastic-surgeon-sentenced-death-murdering-missing-lawyer/story?id=138327475); [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/us/florida-man-sentenced-death-cold-calculated-murder-attorney-body-never-found.amp)).

Kosowski argued the crime was impossible as charged. He questioned the date of death, ABC News reports. We found his argument in that one outlet.

## Why the judge chose death

The judge weighed "aggravating" factors against "mitigating" ones. An aggravating factor is a fact that makes a crime worse. A mitigating factor counts in the defendant's favor.

- **Cold, calculated and premeditated.** The judge found the murder was planned in a cold and calculated way ([ABC News](https://abcnews.com/US/florida-plastic-surgeon-sentenced-death-murdering-missing-lawyer/story?id=138327475); [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/us/florida-man-sentenced-death-cold-calculated-murder-attorney-body-never-found.amp); [ABA Journal](https://abajournal.com/news/article/plastic-surgeon-sentenced-to-death-for-killing-beloved-lawyer)).
- **Financial gain.** ABC News reports the judge also found the murder was done for financial gain. We found that factor in one outlet.
- **The weighing.** The judge ruled the aggravating factors outweighed any mitigating ones ([ABC News](https://abcnews.com/US/florida-plastic-surgeon-sentenced-death-murdering-missing-lawyer/story?id=138327475); [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/us/florida-man-sentenced-death-cold-calculated-murder-attorney-body-never-found.amp)).

Fox News reports the judge first denied a last-minute motion from Kosowski asking the judge to step aside. We found that in one outlet.

## What happens next

The conviction and the death sentence stand. They can be challenged on appeal. Fox News reports the Public Defender's Office was appointed to handle his appeal. ABC News says a public defender will be assigned if he appeals. We have not seen an appeal filing ourselves. Kosowski goes to the Florida Department of Corrections ([ABC News](https://abcnews.com/US/florida-plastic-surgeon-sentenced-death-murdering-missing-lawyer/story?id=138327475)).

No later court date has been set that we have verified.
