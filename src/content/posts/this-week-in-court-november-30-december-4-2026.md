### Wednesday, Dec. 2 — Mario Fernandez Saldana, separate charges (Jacksonville, Florida)

**Time:** Not published. The time has not been publicly verified.

Jared Bridegan was shot and killed in Jacksonville Beach in 2022. A jury found Mario Fernandez Saldana guilty of first-degree murder in August. ### Friday, Dec. 4 — Luigi Mangione, federal sentencing filing due (New York)

**Time:** This is a paper deadline. No one goes to court.

Brian Thompson, the UnitedHealthcare CEO, was shot and killed in Manhattan in December 2024. Luigi Mangione pleaded guilty on Aug. 14 to two federal counts, stalking and cyberstalking that resulted in death. Federal sentencing is set for Friday, Dec. 18, at 11:00 a.m. ET. Judge Margaret Garnett decides the sentence. Our last docket check, on Oct. 21, found the defense's sentencing paper due Dec. 4 and the government's due Dec. 11. The public docket we can read shows nothing newer than a sealed filing on Oct. 30, so we have not re-confirmed these deadlines this week. In the separate New York state case, he has pleaded not guilty and is presumed innocent. The next state date is Dec. 10 at 9:00 a.m. ET.

**What to watch:** Whether the defense paper is filed on time, and whether it is public or sealed.
