*Status as of Oct. 24, 2026, 12:00 PM PT. Court dates can change. Check our [Court Calendar](https://cassiancreed.com/court-calendar/) and its "last updated" date.*

There is no trial date yet in the Charlie Kirk case. Tyler Robinson is charged in Kirk's shooting death. At a hearing on Oct. 23, 2026, the judge heard three very different plans. He did not pick one. He set Monday, Nov. 9, 2026, to rule on a trial schedule. [[1]](#src-1)[[2]](#src-2)

Robinson, 23, has pleaded not guilty. He is presumed innocent. [[4]](#src-4)

## Who was Charlie Kirk?

Charlie Kirk was the husband of Erika Kirk and the father of two young children. He was a co-founder of Turning Point USA. He was shot and killed on Sept. 10, 2025, while speaking at Utah Valley University. [[1]](#src-1)[[7]](#src-7)[[8]](#src-8)

Some reports call him the founder of Turning Point USA. Others say co-founder. We use "co-founder," which fits both reports.

## Has a trial date been set?

No. Judge Tony Graf heard arguments on Oct. 23 but did not set a date. [[1]](#src-1)[[2]](#src-2) He said: "I am not going to run back into my office and type quickly and kick something out." [[1]](#src-1)

He set Nov. 9, 2026, to rule on a trial schedule. FOX 13 reports the lawyers return to court that day at 1 p.m. Mountain time. Only one outlet has reported that time, so treat it as tentative. A trial date may not be set that day. [[2]](#src-2)

## When could the trial start?

Three sides gave three plans. None of them is a set date. [[1]](#src-1)[[2]](#src-2)[[3]](#src-3)

| Who asked | What they asked for |
|---|---|
| Prosecutors | Start Jan. 31, 2028. The trial would last about 17 weeks. They said they could be ready by Oct. 4, 2027. |
| The defense | The third quarter of 2029. The trial would last six to seven months. |
| Erika Kirk's lawyer | October 2027. |

## Why does the defense want 2029?

The defense says it needs time to go through very large evidence files. It also points to a mitigation investigation (gathering facts about a defendant's life that could matter at sentencing), jury selection and a possible study of whether the trial should move to another county. [[1]](#src-1)[[2]](#src-2)

No request to move the trial has been filed, as far as public reports show.

## What did Erika Kirk ask for?

Erika Kirk's lawyer asked for a trial in October 2027. The lawyer pointed to Utah's victims' right to a "speedy disposition," which means a prompt end to the case. [[1]](#src-1)[[2]](#src-2)[[7]](#src-7)

## What is Tyler Robinson charged with?

Robinson is charged with seven counts. The most serious is aggravated murder. [[4]](#src-4)

Prosecutors allege "aggravating circumstances." These include that the victim was targeted. These are claims. A jury has not decided them. [[4]](#src-4)

Reports differ on how Utah labels the aggravated murder count. One says "first-degree felony." Another says "capital felony." We have not read the charging paper, so we do not settle that here.

## Did he plead guilty?

No. He pleaded not guilty to each count on Sept. 1, 2026. He is presumed innocent. There has been no trial, no verdict and no sentence. [[4]](#src-4)

## What does "bound over" mean?

On Sept. 1, 2026, a judge bound Robinson over for trial on all seven counts. [[4]](#src-4)

"Bound over" means a judge found there is enough evidence for the case to go to trial. It is a step in the process. That ruling is not a finding of guilt.

## Is this a death penalty case?

Prosecutors are seeking the death penalty. On June 26, 2026, the judge ruled that the death penalty stays available as an option. [[5]](#src-5) No one can know the outcome. A jury has not heard the case.

## Is there a gag order?

Yes, for the lawyers. A court order limits what the lawyers can say in public about the case. It does not bind the news media. [[5]](#src-5)[[6]](#src-6)

On June 26, 2026, the judge held a deputy county attorney in civil contempt for a public comment about the evidence. [[5]](#src-5)

## Will the trial be on TV?

The judge allowed cameras at the preliminary hearing in July 2026. [[9]](#src-9) Camera rules for the trial itself are not known from public records.

## What happens next?

- **Nov. 9, 2026:** the judge plans to rule on a trial schedule. The 1 p.m. time is tentative. A trial date may not be set that day. [[1]](#src-1)[[2]](#src-2)
- Whether the judge will rule before Nov. 9 is not known.
- After a schedule is set, more pretrial steps are likely, such as jury selection and the venue question. (Theory: this is our reading of the defense's stated plans, not a court order.)

## What is still not known?

- The trial date.
- The confirmed time on Nov. 9.
- The state case number. We have not checked it against a court record.
- The felony label on the aggravated murder count.
- Whether cameras will be allowed at trial.
- What the defense will argue at trial.

We found no federal case. This is a Utah state case in the Fourth District Court in Provo.

**Who is the judge?**
Judge Tony Graf of Utah's Fourth District Court in Provo. [[2]](#src-2)

**How old is Tyler Robinson?**
He is 23. [[4]](#src-4)

**Was he sentenced?**
No. There has been no trial and no verdict.
