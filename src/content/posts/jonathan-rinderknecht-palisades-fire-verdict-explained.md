A federal jury in Los Angeles found Jonathan Rinderknecht guilty on Nov. 20, 2026. It was his second trial. The jury convicted him on two counts tied to the fire that became the January 2025 Palisades Fire. He has not been sentenced. Sentencing is set for March 17, 2027.

## The people and the place first

The Palisades Fire killed 12 people. ABC7 and CBS Los Angeles report it burned more than 23,000 acres. NBC Los Angeles and CBS Los Angeles report more than 6,800 homes and other buildings were lost ([CBS Los Angeles](https://www.cbsnews.com/losangeles/news/jonathan-rinderknecht-found-guilty-in-palisades-fire-case/); [NBC Los Angeles](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/local/palisades-arson-trial-jonathan-rinderknecht/3972494/)).

## What the jury decided

The jury found him guilty of two federal crimes ([ABC7 Los Angeles](https://abc7.com/post/jonathan-rinderknecht-found-guilty-setting-blaze-became-palisades-fire-during-retrial/20060892/); [CBS Los Angeles](https://www.cbsnews.com/losangeles/news/jonathan-rinderknecht-found-guilty-in-palisades-fire-case/)):

- **Destruction of property by means of fire.**
- **Arson affecting property used in interstate commerce.** In plain terms, it covers property used in business that reaches across state lines.

Prosecutors dropped a third charge before the retrial. The first trial had three counts.

How long the jury talked is not clear. ABC7 says the verdict came on the third day. NBC Los Angeles gives two figures, about two and a half days and nearly three days. A juror told CBS Los Angeles it was almost two days.

## Why there were two trials

His first trial ended in a mistrial in June 2026. The jury could not agree. NBC Los Angeles reports 10 of the 12 jurors wanted to acquit him. Prosecutors then got a new indictment with two counts and tried him again. The retrial began Nov. 4 ([NBC Los Angeles](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/local/palisades-arson-trial-jonathan-rinderknecht/3972494/)).

## What prosecutors said

These were the government's arguments. The jury accepted them on the two counts.

- **The first fire.** Prosecutors said he set a small brush fire, the Lachman Fire, just after midnight on Jan. 1, 2025 ([ABC7 Los Angeles](https://abc7.com/post/jonathan-rinderknecht-found-guilty-setting-blaze-became-palisades-fire-during-retrial/20060892/); [CBS Los Angeles](https://www.cbsnews.com/losangeles/news/jonathan-rinderknecht-found-guilty-in-palisades-fire-case/)).
- **The second fire.** They said that fire smoldered underground for days. Strong winds brought it back on Jan. 7, and it grew into the Palisades Fire ([ABC7 Los Angeles](https://abc7.com/post/jonathan-rinderknecht-found-guilty-setting-blaze-became-palisades-fire-during-retrial/20060892/); [NBC Los Angeles](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/local/palisades-arson-trial-jonathan-rinderknecht/3972494/)).
- **The evidence they used.** Phone location data, fire-camera video, records of his ChatGPT use, and statements they said were false ([NBC Los Angeles](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/local/palisades-arson-trial-jonathan-rinderknecht/3972494/); [CBS Los Angeles](https://www.cbsnews.com/losangeles/news/jonathan-rinderknecht-found-guilty-in-palisades-fire-case/)).

## What the defense said

His lawyer, Steve Haney, said the case was circumstantial. He said no one saw the fire start. He argued Rinderknecht was a witness to the fire, not the person who set it ([NBC Los Angeles](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/local/palisades-arson-trial-jonathan-rinderknecht/3972494/); [CBS Los Angeles](https://www.cbsnews.com/losangeles/news/jonathan-rinderknecht-found-guilty-in-palisades-fire-case/)). His father told NBC Los Angeles and CBS Los Angeles he still believes his son is innocent.

The jury did not agree with the defense on these two counts. The verdict stands unless a court later sets it aside.

## What happens next

- **Sentencing:** March 17, 2027, before Judge Anne Hwang, per the U.S. Attorney's Office case page. ABC7 and NBC Los Angeles report the same date.
- **The possible sentence:** ABC7 reports prosecutors put the range at a minimum of five years and up to 40 years in federal prison. CBS Los Angeles reports the same range. The judge decides. We do not predict a sentence.
- **An appeal:** He can appeal the conviction. We have not seen an appeal filed yet.
