**Status as of November 12, 2026:** No big trial is in session next week. This week's trials ended with verdicts: a jury found Matthew Farwell guilty on both federal counts, a jury found Naresh Bhatt guilty of first-degree murder and two related counts, and a jury found Markies Conway guilty of capital murder (Court Calendar, verified Nov. 11). Next week is hearings and one sentencing. Here is who, when, and what to watch.

*One of these cases involves the deaths of children. All times are local court time, with Pacific time next to them. Courts can change dates and times at any time. Many times below are not confirmed by a court, and we say so. We list a livestream only when a court or network confirms one, so none are listed here.*

### Monday, Nov. 16 — Lindsay Clancy, hearing after her mistrial (Plymouth, Massachusetts)

**Time:** Boston 25 News reported on Nov. 2 that the hearing is at 9 a.m. (6 a.m. PT). It did not name a time zone, and we have not seen the court's own notice. Treat the time as tentative.

Lindsay Clancy is charged with three counts of first-degree murder in the deaths of her three young children in Duxbury in January 2023. She has pleaded not guilty and is presumed innocent. Her first trial ended in a mistrial on Sept. 4 when the jury could not agree. A mistrial is not a verdict. On Nov. 9, Judge William Sullivan denied the defense request to dismiss the case, ABC News reported. He said the deadlock meant a new trial does not break the rule against trying someone twice for the same crime. No new trial date is set. Newsweek reported that a retrial could come as early as March 2027. That is not a set date.

**What to watch:** Whether the judge sets a retrial date.

### Wednesday, Nov. 18 — Gabriela "Gabbie" Gonzalez, Francisco Gonzalez and Kai Cordrey, hearing (Los Angeles)

**Time:** Not publicly verified. We do not have a start time, department or docket number.

Prosecutors say the three took part in a plot to kill the singer Jack Avery. Reports list charges of attempted murder, conspiracy to commit murder and solicitation of murder. All three pleaded not guilty on Sept. 14 and are presumed innocent. Our Court Calendar lists this date as a preliminary hearing, citing Court TV. That is a hearing where a judge decides whether there is enough evidence to go forward. LA Magazine reported only that the three "will be back in court on November 18."

**What to watch:** Whether the hearing goes ahead and whether the judge sends the case to trial.

### Thursday, Nov. 19 — Isabelle Johnson, pretrial hearing (Walton County, Florida)

**Time:** Not publicly verified. The court is in DeFuniak Springs, which is on Central time.

Isabelle Johnson is accused in the death of Jason Coulthart of Niceville, Florida. WJHG reported in August that she faces an open count of murder and "will be back in court on November 19th for her pretrial." Our Court Calendar, citing a Sept. 15 Court TV report, lists amended charges: second-degree murder, abuse of a dead human body and evidence tampering. She has pleaded not guilty and is presumed innocent. A judge did not grant her bond in August.

**What to watch:** What the court sets next. The reports we found do not say what this hearing will decide.

Full details: https://cassiancreed.com/court-calendar/

### Friday, Nov. 20 — Tomasz Kosowski, sentencing (Clearwater, Florida)

**Time:** FOX 13 reported on Oct. 8 that sentencing is set for 1:30 p.m. ET (10:30 a.m. PT). We could not open the clerk's docket today, so the time is tentative.

Steven Cozzi, a Largo attorney, disappeared in March 2023. His body has never been found. Kosowski, a former plastic surgeon, waived a jury and was tried by a judge. On Sept. 23, Judge Joseph Bulone found him guilty of first-degree murder. The judge held a sentencing-evidence hearing, called a Spencer hearing, on Oct. 8. He has not yet imposed a sentence. He will choose between death and life in prison.

**What to watch:** The sentence.
