**October 4 edition · Week ahead: October 5–11 · Sources checked October 3, 2026.**

A court date tells you when to pay attention. Understanding the proceeding tells you what to watch for. Here are four selected cases with upcoming settings, the question before the court, and the limits of what we can currently verify.

Settings can change. The [current Court Calendar](/court-calendar/?utm_source=website&utm_medium=twic&utm_campaign=twic_20261004&utm_content=court_calendar_return) carries source links and verification notes. Unresolved charges remain allegations; people facing them are presumed innocent unless proved guilty.

## Monday, October 5: Naresh Bhatt — what does the docket actually confirm?

The [Prince William Circuit Court criminal docket](https://pwcaws.pwcva.gov/assets/2022-10/CRIMINAL%20DOCKET.pdf), headed October 5, lists Naresh Bhatt at **10 a.m. Eastern in Courtroom 5**, under the code **JURY**. The courtroom assignment is preliminary. The code establishes a jury proceeding; it does not establish precisely which stage of jury selection will occur or any outcome.

Bhatt faces murder and other charges in the disappearance of Mamta Kafle Bhatt. He is presumed innocent. Watch for the court’s next procedural step, and check the docket again before attending.

## Monday, October 5: Tony Wiggins — recommendation and sentence are separate

A jury found Tony Lee Wiggins Jr. guilty of three counts of first-degree murder on September 29. [FOX 13’s October 2 courtroom report](https://www.fox13news.com/news/tj-wiggins-penalty-phase-resumes-jury-weighs-death-penalty-life-sentence) says both sides have rested in the penalty phase and jurors will return **Monday morning for final instructions and deliberations**. An exact start time has not been verified, and this schedule comes from reporting rather than a current clerk docket reviewed here.

The next question concerns punishment. A jury’s penalty recommendation and the judge’s final sentence are separate steps. Neither a recommendation nor a final sentence is recorded in this edition.

## Wednesday, October 7: Nick Reiner — a motions hearing before trial

The [Los Angeles County District Attorney’s September 15 update](https://da.lacounty.gov/media/news/district-attorney-hochman-announces-death-penalty-won-t-be-sought-reiner-murder-case) lists an **October 7 motions hearing in Department 108** at the Clara Shortridge Foltz Criminal Justice Center. A start time has not been publicly verified here.

Reiner has pleaded not guilty to murder charges in the deaths of Rob Reiner and Michele Singer Reiner. He remains presumed innocent. Watch for rulings on the motions and any further schedule: a motions hearing does not itself determine guilt or impose a sentence.

## Thursday, October 8: Tomasz Kosowski — arguments before sentencing

The [published courtroom-video transcript](https://www.rev.com/transcripts/fl-v-tomasz-koswoski-day-7-and-verdict) records the judge announcing a **Spencer hearing for October 8 at 9 a.m. Eastern**. This is an announced setting; a current filed notice and courtroom have not been independently verified here. Reconfirm with the clerk before attending.

Kosowski was found guilty of first-degree murder in Steven Cozzi’s death. The coming hearing concerns arguments before final sentencing. It is not a new guilt trial, and the announced hearing is not a sentence already imposed.
