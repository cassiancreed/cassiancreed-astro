This is the shortest case file on this site, and it is short on purpose.

**William "Billy" Treeby**, 26, from Kent, died in Dubai in June 2026. **Brooke George**, 23, also from Kent, has been detained in the United Arab Emirates since **June 22, 2026**.

No date of death has been published for Treeby, and no cause of death has been stated by any authority. That absence is not a detail — it is the reason this page is as short as it is.

Almost everything else that has been published about this case comes from people advocating for one side of it. We are not going to pass that along as though a court had established it.

## What Is Known About William Treeby and Brooke George

Widely circulated reporting says Brooke George has been charged with murder. **That claim does not come from UAE authorities or from a court.** It traces to an advocacy organization campaigning on her behalf, and to her family. The same is true of the account that she acted in self-defense, and of the descriptions of how she has been treated in custody. Those may all turn out to be accurate. None of them has been confirmed by anyone with the authority to confirm it.

On the other side, friends and family of William Treeby have given a contrasting account. That is not confirmed by an authority either.

What *is* officially confirmed is narrow: the Foreign Office is in contact with the UAE authorities and is supporting the family. Reporters who approached Dubai Police and the UAE's diplomatic mission in Britain have reported no substantive response.

So the honest position is this. William Treeby is dead. Brooke George is in custody. **No authority has stated on any record we can read what connects those facts, whether anything does, or what she is alleged to have done. She is entitled to the presumption of innocence in full measure.**

Our [court calendar](/court-calendar/) carries this as a record entry rather than a scheduled proceeding, because there is no proceeding to list. We can read an American docket. We generally cannot read an Emirati one, and we will not narrate a legal system we cannot check.

## What Would Change This Page

- **A charge confirmed by UAE authorities or a court.** That would establish a legal status where there is currently none.
- **A scheduled proceeding on a public record.** That would move this from a record entry to a calendar entry.
- **An official UAE statement of any kind** about the cause of death or the basis for the detention.
- **A substantive FCDO statement** beyond consular contact.

Until one of those arrives, this page will say what it says now.
