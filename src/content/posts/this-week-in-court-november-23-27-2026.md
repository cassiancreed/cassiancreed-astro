### All week — Dwain Hall trial continues (Marion County, Florida)

**Time:** Not publicly verified. No daily start time or courtroom has been published.

Dwain Hall is charged with first-degree murder and kidnapping in the death of Sonia Exelby, 32, of England. He has pleaded not guilty and is presumed innocent. The charges are allegations. WCJB reported that opening statements and testimony began on Tuesday, Nov. 17. ### Monday, Nov. 23 — Corie Walsh, pretrial hearing (Will County, Illinois)

**Time:** 9:30 a.m. CT (7:30 a.m. PT), Room 405, per the Will County court schedule. We last checked that schedule on Nov. 15.

Corie Walsh of Frankfort, Illinois, is charged with three counts of first-degree murder in the death of her 2-year-old son. She pleaded not guilty on Sept. 24 and is presumed innocent. This hearing was moved from Oct. 26. News reports said the new date gives both sides more time to go through evidence. A pretrial hearing does not decide guilt.

**What to watch:** Whether the court sets a schedule for the next steps.

### Monday, Nov. 23 — Kimberly Sullivan, hearing (Waterbury, Connecticut)

**Time:** Not publicly verified. News reports give the date but no time.

Kimberly Sullivan is accused of holding her stepson captive in their home for more than 20 years. She has pleaded not guilty and is presumed innocent. The charges are allegations. They include kidnapping, first-degree assault, unlawful restraint and cruelty to persons. NBC Connecticut reported on Oct. 20 that a judge is reviewing sealed records to decide what can be used at trial. It also reported that the defense wants a 12-person jury instead of six, and that questions about the jury panel and expert witnesses will be decided at the Nov. 23 hearing. WJAR reported that the trial is expected in February. Nov. 23 is not a trial date.

**What to watch:** Whether the judge rules on jury size. Our last check of this case was Oct. 21, so look for newer news before you rely on this date.

Full details: https://cassiancreed.com/court-calendar/

### Tuesday, Nov. 24 — Karen Read, civil hearing (Plymouth County, Massachusetts)

**Time:** 10:00 a.m. ET (7:00 a.m. PT), per the MassCourts docket, checked Nov. 11 and Nov. 15. The docket shows the session but no room number.

This is a civil lawsuit, not a criminal case. The family of Boston police officer John O'Keefe is suing Karen Read and two Canton bars over his death. A jury acquitted Read of murder and manslaughter in June 2025. No court has found her liable in this lawsuit. Judge Mark Gildea is hearing it. A Rule 56 hearing is where a judge hears a request to decide all or part of a case without a trial. The docket does not say which side asked, or what the judge will decide.

**What to watch:** Whether the judge rules that day or takes it under review.

Full details: https://cassiancreed.com/court-calendar/
