South Carolina's parole board said no to Susan Smith on Nov. 19, 2026. It was her second request for release, and her second denial in two years. She stays in prison on her life sentences. She can ask again in about two years.

## Two boys first

In October 1994, Susan Smith let her car roll into John D. Long Lake in Union, South Carolina. Her two young sons were strapped inside. One was 3. The other was 14 months old ([SC Daily Gazette](https://scdailygazette.com/2026/11/19/parole-again-denied-for-susan-smith-32-years-after-she-killed-her-2-sons/)). Both died.

They were children. We keep their names off this page.

## What the board decided

- **The vote:** The board voted to deny parole. SC Daily Gazette and WSOC-TV both report the vote was unanimous. SC Daily Gazette reports one member stepped aside because she was once a warden at a prison where Smith was held. Only that outlet reported the step-aside.
- **Where Smith was:** She spoke to the board by video from Leath Correctional Institution ([WSOC-TV](https://www.wsoctv.com/news/local/susan-smith-denied-parole-second-time-2-years/SOFUNI7WDBBNZG4WZ6ODZR6FDI/); [SC Daily Gazette](https://scdailygazette.com/2026/11/19/parole-again-denied-for-susan-smith-32-years-after-she-killed-her-2-sons/); [WLOS](https://wlos.com/news/local/susan-smith-second-parole-hearing-2026-south-carolina-sons-murder)).
- **The reasons:** SC Daily Gazette reports the board named the risk to the community, the seriousness of the crime, and Smith's attitude toward it. We found that list in one outlet only.

## Who spoke at the hearing

**Susan Smith** apologized. Both SC Daily Gazette and WSOC-TV report she told the board she "dropped to my lowest" when the car went into the lake. Her lawyer spoke about her mental health and depression ([WSOC-TV](https://www.wsoctv.com/news/local/susan-smith-denied-parole-second-time-2-years/SOFUNI7WDBBNZG4WZ6ODZR6FDI/)).

**David Smith**, the boys' father, asked the board to keep her in prison. He said release would be an injustice ([SC Daily Gazette](https://scdailygazette.com/2026/11/19/parole-again-denied-for-susan-smith-32-years-after-she-killed-her-2-sons/); [WSOC-TV](https://www.wsoctv.com/news/local/susan-smith-denied-parole-second-time-2-years/SOFUNI7WDBBNZG4WZ6ODZR6FDI/)).

**Tommy Pope** prosecuted the case in 1995. He is now a state lawmaker. SC Daily Gazette reports he wants to change the parole rules so people like Smith wait longer between hearings. Only that outlet reported his plan.

## Why she can ask for parole at all

A jury convicted Susan Smith of two counts of murder in July 1995. Prosecutors asked for the death penalty. The jury chose life in prison instead ([WLOS](https://wlos.com/news/local/susan-smith-second-parole-hearing-2026-south-carolina-sons-murder); [SC Daily Gazette](https://scdailygazette.com/2026/11/19/parole-again-denied-for-susan-smith-32-years-after-she-killed-her-2-sons/)).

At that time, a life sentence in South Carolina allowed a parole review after 30 years. SC Daily Gazette reports lawmakers ended parole for life sentences the next year. That later change did not apply to her. So she became eligible in 2024. The board denied her that year too, on Nov. 20, 2024 ([WLOS](https://wlos.com/news/local/susan-smith-second-parole-hearing-2026-south-carolina-sons-murder)).

Her conviction is final. A parole hearing does not reopen the verdict. It only asks whether she should be released.

## When she can ask again

SC Daily Gazette reports she can reapply every two years. WLOS reports that people serving time for violent crimes generally get another review two years after a denial. That points to about November 2028. We found that timing in two outlets. We have not seen the board set an exact date.

## Questions people ask

### Was Susan Smith denied parole in 2026?

Yes. The South Carolina Board of Paroles and Pardons denied her request on Nov. 19, 2026. SC Daily Gazette and WSOC-TV report the vote was unanimous. It was her second denial. The first came in November 2024.

### When is Susan Smith's next parole hearing?

No date is set. Two outlets report she can ask again about two years after a denial, which points to about November 2028.

### What was Susan Smith convicted of?

Two counts of murder, in July 1995. A jury gave her life in prison, not the death penalty.

### Can lawmakers change her parole schedule?

SC Daily Gazette reports a lawmaker wants to lengthen the wait between hearings. No change has passed that we have seen. Whether a new rule would apply to her is not known yet.

### Where is Susan Smith now?

She is held at Leath Correctional Institution in South Carolina. She spoke to the board by video from there.
