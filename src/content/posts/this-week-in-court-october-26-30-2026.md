Two trials go on this week. In a third case, a jury reached its verdict on Friday, Oct. 23. There is also a hearing in Illinois, a federal sentencing, a busy day in one Florida case, and a filing deadline. Here is who, when, and what to watch.

*One of these cases involves the death of a child. All times are local court time, with Pacific time next to them. Courts can change dates and times at any time. Check the Court Calendar before you tune in. We list a livestream only when a court or network confirms one, so none are listed here.*

### All week — Matthew Farwell trial continues (Boston, federal court)

**Time:** Court has been starting at 8:45 a.m. ET (5:45 a.m. PT), per the judge's trial notes. Next week's times are not confirmed.

This trial is about the 2021 death of Sandra Birchmore, 23, who was pregnant. Matthew Farwell, a former Stoughton, Massachusetts, police detective, is charged with killing a witness and killing a child in utero. He has pleaded not guilty. Chief Judge Denise J. Casper presides. On Thursday, Oct. 22, the man officials say was the father of her unborn baby was expected to testify. No verdict has been reported.

**What to watch:** Whether the prosecution rests, and whether the defense calls witnesses.

### Verdict — Faith Hedgepeth case (Durham, North Carolina)

**Time:** The jury's verdict came on Friday, Oct. 23. No appeal court date has been reported.

Faith Hedgepeth, 19, was a student at UNC-Chapel Hill. She was found dead in her apartment in September 2012. On Friday, Oct. 23, 2026, a Durham County jury found Miguel Salguero-Olivares guilty of four charges: first-degree murder, first-degree rape, first-degree sexual offense and first-degree burglary. He was sentenced to life in prison without parole for the murder, plus more prison time on the other charges, WRAL reported. WRAL also reported that he is appealing. His convictions stand while the appeal goes on. Judge Keith Gregory presided.

**What to watch:** Any appeal court date. None has been reported.

### Tuesday, Oct. 27 — Alon, Oren and Tal Alexander, sentencing (New York, federal court)

**Time:** 10:00 a.m. ET (7:00 a.m. PT), Courtroom 26A, per a court order.

In March, a federal jury found the three brothers guilty of sex-trafficking and related charges. Judge Valerie E. Caproni moved sentencing from Oct. 6 to Oct. 27. Her order says she will not delay it again. Prosecutors have asked for life in prison. The defense has asked for much less. Survivors may speak in court.

### Tuesday, Oct. 27 — Jared Bridegan case, two settings (Jacksonville, Florida)

**Time:** Not confirmed by the court. Check before you tune in.

Jared Bridegan was shot and killed in Jacksonville Beach in 2022. In August, a jury found Mario Fernandez Saldana guilty of first-degree murder. His lawyers have asked for a new trial. News4JAX reported that his sentencing is set for Oct. 27. In Florida, that charge carries a mandatory life sentence. An older report also listed a 9:00 a.m. ET (6:00 a.m. PT) pretrial hearing that day for Shanna Gardner, Jared's ex-wife. Newer reports do not mention it, so we treat it as unconfirmed. Gardner has pleaded not guilty and is presumed innocent. Her trial is set for May 2027.

**What to watch:** Whether sentencing goes forward, and any ruling on the new-trial request.

### Friday, Oct. 30 — Luigi Mangione, filing deadline (New York state case)

**Time:** This is a paper deadline. No one goes to court.

Brian Thompson, the UnitedHealthcare CEO, was shot and killed in Manhattan in December 2024. Luigi Mangione pleaded guilty in August to two federal counts. His federal sentencing is set for Dec. 18. In the separate New York state case, he has pleaded not guilty to murder and is presumed innocent. His lawyers say a state trial would try him twice for the same act. That is called double jeopardy. Oct. 30 is their last day to answer the prosecutors in writing. The next state court date is Dec. 10.

**What to watch:** Whether the judge sets a date to rule.
